Skippers leave at about the same second whether the video is 30s or 120s. Skip-second data is a 10% sample of 1.6 billion events, so read it directionally: runtime is not what drives the skip, the opening seconds are.

4 · Payoff

The value shows up later, and it's worth the wait

Long video converts fewer people on day one, but the ones who install spend more over time. Short video still leads on raw return. The gap is what closes, so judge long-form on Day 28, not Day 7.

Short is still ahead on the raw numbers at both marks. The story is the slope: long-form goes from 0.57x of short at Day 7 to 0.64x at Day 28 and keeps building. Day 28 is the fair horizon for judging long-form, not the day it overtakes short.
